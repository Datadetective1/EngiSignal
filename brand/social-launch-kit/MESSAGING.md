# EngiSignal — Social Messaging

Written in the house voice from [`BRAND.md`](../../BRAND.md) §4: plain, specific,
short sentences, no adjective doing a job a number should do.

Two constraints shaped every line below, and they should shape every line that
replaces them:

- **No invented customers, savings figures, partnerships or endorsements.** Nothing
  here claims a result. Where a number appears it is an illustration of the method,
  labelled as one.
- **No claim that a connector exists.** Connector interfaces exist; no connector is
  implemented. The posts talk about importing data, which is what the product does.

## 2. LinkedIn "About" section

> EngiSignal is an Engineering Software Intelligence platform. It turns the usage,
> license, contract and organizational data your engineering organization already
> produces into renewal, cost and capacity decisions you can defend.
>
> Engineering software is one of the largest line items in a technical organization
> and one of the least measured. Renewals arrive on a date, not on evidence.
> Quantities get carried forward because nobody can say what the right number is.
> Named seats sit idle for quarters. Denials go unrecorded, so the case for more
> capacity is as thin as the case for less.
>
> EngiSignal does not create data. It makes the data you already have mean
> something:
>
> — Peak concurrent demand measured against what you are entitled to
> — Recommended quantities with the headroom assumption stated

> — Renewal positions produced before the renewal, not after

> says so and says why. That matters when a recommendation has to survive a
> conversation with a vendor, a finance partner and an engineering director in the
> same meeting.
>
> Built for engineering, aerospace, manufacturing and technical operations teams
> running CAD, CAE, EDA, simulation and PLM portfolios.
>
> Currently taking organizations into a 30-Day Engineering Software Intelligence
> Pilot: connect, analyze, validate, decide.
>
> engisignal.com

### Post 2 — The problem

*Pair with:* `05-post-templates/engisignal-post-statement-1200x1200.png`

> Three questions that come up in every engineering software renewal, and rarely
> have an evidence-backed answer:
>
> 1. What was our actual peak concurrent demand — not our average, not our seat
> count?
> 2. How many named users have not launched the product in 90 days?
> 3. If headcount moves 10%, what does demand do?
>
> The data to answer all three usually exists. It sits in license server logs,
> contract PDFs and an HR export, in three different formats, owned by three
> different people, and nobody has the week it takes to reconcile them before the
> renewal date.
>
> That reconciliation is the product.

---

### Post 3 — What it does

*Pair with:* `05-post-templates/engisignal-what-it-is-1600x900.png`

> What "Engineering Software Intelligence" actually means.
>
> In: usage and denials, named users and their activity, contracts and entitlements,
> cost and unit price, headcount and projects.
>
> Out: recommended quantity, P95 peak demand, reclaim candidates, renewal position,
> forecast demand.
>
> The work in between is normalization and measurement, not magic. Feature names
> differ across vendors. Contracts express entitlement in four different units.
> Concurrent and named licensing have to be measured differently. Denials mean
> something only when you know the entitlement at the moment they occurred.
>
> Getting that right is what makes the output defensible. Getting it wrong is why
> most license reports get ignored.

### Post 5 — Principle

*Pair with:* `05-post-templates/engisignal-feature-ask-engisignal-1600x900.png`

> A rule we build to: no number appears without its derivation.
>
> It sounds like a nice-to-have. It is the whole product.
>
> A license recommendation is a claim you will have to defend — to a vendor with a
> commercial interest in a bigger number, to a finance partner who wants a smaller
> one, and to an engineering director who will remember it if a queue forms. "The
> tool said 318" does not survive that room. "P95 daily peak demand was 275 against
> 400 entitled, and 318 holds a headroom buffer through the forecast period, here is
> the daily series" does.
>
> So the derivation ships with the number. Where a figure is uncertain, the product
> says so and says why. Where a unit price is missing, it says "no unit price
> recorded" instead of showing $0.
>
> Confidence is worth nothing without its reasons.

## Posting notes

- **Numbers in these posts are illustrative of the method.** The 275/400/318 example
  is the worked example from `BRAND.md` §4. Do not re-frame it as a customer result.
- **Do not add vendor logos** to any post. Vendor names are set in type; their
  appearance implies no affiliation or endorsement. Where a post is likely to be
  read as implying a relationship, include the disclosure from `config/brand.ts`:
  *Product names, logos and brands are property of their respective owners. Their
  appearance does not imply affiliation or endorsement.*
- **The demo organization is synthetic** and is labelled as such wherever it appears.
  If you screenshot the product, keep that label in frame.
- LinkedIn post 4 and X post 4 both ask for replies. Only publish them if someone is
  watching the inbox.
